About Us: The Team

RTX stands for our names: Riley Hagel, Tristen Balsiger and Xavier Gowin.

As of May 11, 2026, we are freshmen at Spokane Valley Tech (SVT) high school, and every year our school puts on a tradeshow where we must find a problem and make a solution.

Our problem is rust in Power Take-Off (PTO) Driveshafts on tractors. PTO shafts are the metal shafts with universal joints on the ends that connec an engines to differentials, which powers the wheels. They are also used to connect tractor engines to external farming implements like mower decks. The tractor has a separate clutch for the shaft, and the shaft telescopes.
